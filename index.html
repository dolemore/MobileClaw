The long story

Your phone has a brain it isn't using. MobileClaw wakes it up.

Your flagship has more memory than a laptop from a few years ago, and it spends its day showing icons. MobileClaw turns it into an assistant that knows you, runs AI on the phone itself and works for you even when it's in your pocket.

  1. 01 · The core

    An AI that lives on your phone

    Gemma 4 and other models run right on the device: no internet, no account, and nothing leaves the phone.

    Want more power? Add a cloud AI: sign in to OpenRouter, use your own key, or point it at your own server. MobileClaw picks the right model for each task.

  2. 02 · Connections

    Reach it from anywhere

    Telegram bridge: message your phone from across the world. It answers, and it acts.

    Drive it from outside: a private local API lets n8n, Home Assistant or your own scripts give it commands. A token made on the phone keeps it locked.

    MCP: connect an MCP server and the assistant gains its tools on the spot. Tools that only read run freely; anything else asks you first.

    Your computer's sidekick: the phone becomes a Bluetooth keyboard and trackpad, and sends files straight to your PC over Wi-Fi.

  3. 03 · On its own

    It works while you live

    Automations from one sentence: say “When I get to work, put the phone on silent” and it's set up.

    Scheduled agent tasks: “Every morning at 8, summarize the news” arrives as a notification and on Telegram.

    Ready-made agents and skills: starter skills, expert personas, or your own agents with their own instructions and model. They plan their own steps, and nothing outward happens without your “yes”.

    Your files sort themselves: photos and documents are grouped by trip, place and date. You see the plan first, and one tap undoes it.

  4. 04 · Memory

    A second brain that remembers for you

    Notes it keeps and finds by meaning, not just by keyword, with an embedding model running on the phone.

    It knows your normal, so it notices the day that isn't: “You're usually at work on Fridays. Today you're home. Is everything all right?”

    Your morning in one sentence, the notifications that matter on top, and bills caught before they're due.

    “What I know about you” deletes the evidence underneath, not just the label.

  5. 05 · The surface

    A home screen you shape

    Two glass panels float on the home screen. Drag each one to any edge: left, right, top or bottom. At the top or bottom it turns into a slim bar.

    Tap a panel and it opens into widgets: AI, notifications, calendar, apps, music and weather, with live search.

    The second panel is a shortcut to the AI: chat, the phone's own model, the AI connection, the automation builder, the second brain and Telegram, one tap each.

    Pick its color from seven glass tones, set how much it dims the wallpaper, and choose which widgets it shows. The same glass style can dress the lock screen.

  6. 06 · At home

    Your home, without the cloud

    It finds the smart devices on your Wi-Fi by itself: TVs, Roku, Xiaomi and Roborock, Kasa, Shelly, Tasmota.

    It switches plugs, lights and shutters directly, runs a Valetudo robot vacuum and wakes your PC. If the phone has an IR blaster, it turns on the TV too.

    For everything else it presses the buttons your other apps already offer, like a vacuum's “Start cleaning” or a plug's shortcut. It works with Home Assistant too.

  7. 07 · On the road

    Turn it sideways: it's a car

    A full-screen cockpit with its own map and spoken turn-by-turn directions. Search “pharmacy” and the nearest one comes first.

    A dashcam that starts recording by itself above 15 km/h and plays live in a corner of the screen. Clips stay on the phone.

    A cabin watch, a road-roughness meter, big buttons for music and calls, and only the notifications worth a glance.

  8. 08 · Plug-ins

What it does

One launcher, six jobs

Pick a row — the screen beside it shows the real thing, straight from the Play Store listing.

  1. Ask or speak your request; the answer comes from the phone first and from the cloud only when you ask for it. Chat and voice, no account required.

  2. GGUF models on CPU through llama.cpp, .litertlm models on GPU through Google's LiteRT-LM, plus semantic note recall with EmbeddingGemma. Eight cloud providers are an optional, stateless boost.

  3. Say “when charging turn on do not disturb” or “at 22:00 dim the screen” — MobileClaw builds the trigger-action macro for you. No scripting, no Tasker profiles.

  4. Simple Mode with big buttons and a one-tap family call for elders, a parent-PIN-protected Socratic study coach for kids, and a landscape cockpit for the dashboard.

  5. Drive the TV over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, and the plugs, blinds and robot vacuums the phone discovers at home — from the same home screen.

  6. Quick Settings tiles for SOS, spirit level, dice and more, plus a gallery that files receipts, documents and screenshots on-device with dry-run preview and one-tap undo.

Privacy

The phone keeps its own diary

MobileClaw replaces your home screen, which means it sees your usage — so the default answer is that nothing leaves the device.

  • Offline by default The full assistant runs in airplane mode. Cloud models are opt-in, per request, and never required for the launcher to work.
  • No ads, no accounts Nothing to sign up for, nothing to unsubscribe from, no advertising SDKs. The Play listing is the whole business model.
  • No child tracking Kid Mode collects nothing about children. The parent PIN protects the mode, not a profile — there is no profile.
  • Stateless cloud bridge When you do use it, the Google Cloud Run bridge speaks MCP and OpenAI-compatible JSON, scales to zero, and keeps no conversation history.

Under the hood

Hybrid architecture: on-device AI + Google Cloud Run

Modern flagship phones (6–12 GB RAM) run 3B–4B parameter models entirely offline inside the launcher. But millions of older spare Android phones sitting in drawers only have 2–4 GB of RAM. To leave no device behind, MobileClaw pairs its on-device runtime with a stateless, scale-to-zero Google Cloud Run microservice speaking Model Context Protocol (MCP JSON-RPC 2.0) and OpenAI-compatible /v1/chat/completions: 

[Android Phone: MobileClaw Launcher (com.la1ncher)]
 ├── Tier 0 (Instant Rules): Time, battery, app launch, DND, alarms (<5 ms, 0 RAM)
 ├── Tier 1 (On-Device AI, 6GB+ phones):
 │    ├── LiteRT-LM (GPU .litertlm: Gemma 4 E2B, Qwen2.5 1.5B + EmbeddingGemma 270M)
 │    └── llama.cpp (CPU arm64 GGUF, Q8_0 KV cache, auto-unloads on Android onTrimMemory)
 └── Tier 2 (Low-RAM Upcycled Phones → Google Cloud Run @ getmobileclaw.com):
      ├── POST /mcp (Model Context Protocol JSON-RPC 2.0)
      │    ├── socratic_study_hint   (step-by-step kid study guidance without spoilers)
      │    ├── elder_care_checkin    (structured daily memory & reminder summary)
      │    └── recent_earthquakes    (live USGS seismic safety check near user/family)
      └── POST /v1/chat/completions  (Stateless Cloud Run AI relay for Care & Kid modes)
